# 0010 — Separate video direction from conditioning and assembly

Status: implemented, pending human preview review.

The existing reference preview repeated a single short motion clip under persistent bilingual panels. Its technical validity did not demonstrate an engaging film. Introduce a Video Director that consumes the shared Brief and an observed object catalog, emits semantic actions and a viewer payoff, and delegates frame arithmetic to a deterministic editor. Native director output demonstrated arithmetic failures, so timing cannot be an LLM responsibility. Resolve photo conditioning internally; remove manual endpoint choreography from the brief-led flow. Assemble unique motion shots alongside explicitly photographic camera edits, sparse separate-language text and actual scored audio. Store immutable boards and all input hashes before rendering, retain provenance and AI disclosure, and never turn crop approval into post approval. Missing footage blocks a finished film; explicit animatics remain available. Check within-shot fidelity rather than similarity across deliberate subject cuts. Pacing thresholds are experiment presets; human preference and audience uplift remain unmeasured. Legacy render recipes stay supported. Follow-on scope is contemporary licensed B-roll and a labeled human comparison, not automatic posting or an uncalibrated beauty judge.
