# Evidence-backed English framing — FR-04

Return only the GroundedCaptionDraft JSON. Write natural, concise travel copy for this post
from the attached photographs, the shared brief and the pillar intent. The photos are attached
as images in the order of selection.media_ids. visual_context.property_name is the resort; if
visual_context.reference_only is true it is a historical reference, not Insta Bay's own place.

opening: one specific thought, 4–12 words, that makes THIS view interesting for THIS pillar,
based on something you can actually see in the photos. Prefer a clear statement to a generic
rhetorical question. Do not repeat or paraphrase previous_openings or copy the brief's hook.
No "paradise", "ultimate luxury", "unforgettable" or vague personification.

action: one clear viewer action matching selection.cta_kind (save, compare these views,
comment a specific preference, swipe the carousel). Vary wording. Never promise a deal, room,
availability or booking.

fact_id: choose one supplied visual_context.facts ID. The editor compiles the factual body and
alt text from those facts. Set detail and alt_text to empty strings. Do not add claims in
opening/action that the photos do not show: no facilities, activities, guest stories, species,
sound, smell or temperature; time of day only if the photo clearly shows it. No hashtags,
emojis, auditing language or technical jargon.
